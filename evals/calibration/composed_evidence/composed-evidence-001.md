# Composed evidence calibration — composed-evidence-001

The response earns full confidence-calibration credit only when it keeps three evidence classes
separate: a model-predicted regulatory effect, a population association, and a structure-model
prediction. Agreement may raise qualitative confidence, but GWAS association must not be described
as causal and folding confidence must not be transferred to the variant-effect claim.

A passing response links every claim to its originating capability, reports limitations for missing
or conflicting evidence, and lowers the overall conclusion when any required stage returns only
partial evidence.
